## Getting Started

### First Launch

When you first launch Qash Compass, you'll go through a quick setup process:

1. **Welcome Screen**: Introduction to key features
2. **Currency Selection**: Choose your primary currency
3. **Date Format**: Select your preferred date format
4. **Data Security**: Optionally enable encryption for your financial data

### Main Interface

The application interface consists of:

- **Top Menu Bar**: Access to all features and settings
- **Side Navigation**: Quick access to main sections
- **Dashboard**: Overview of your financial status
- **Main Content Area**: Displays selected section content

## Dashboard Overview

Your dashboard provides a quick snapshot of your finances:

- **Account Balances**: Total balance across all accounts
- **Monthly Summary**: Income vs. Expenses for current month
- **Budget Status**: Progress toward budget goals
- **Recent Transactions**: Latest income and expenses
- **Spending by Category**: Visual breakdown of expenses
- **Net Worth Trend**: Historical view of financial growth

## Managing Accounts

### Adding an Account

1. Navigate to **Accounts** in the side menu
2. Click **"+ Add Account"**
3. Select account type:
   - **Checking Account**
   - **Savings Account**
   - **Credit Card**
   - **Cash**
   - **Investment**
4. Enter account details:
   - Account name
   - Initial balance
   - Currency (if different from default)
   - Account number (optional)
   - Bank name (optional)
5. Click **"Save"**

### Editing an Account

1. Go to **Accounts**
2. Click on the account you want to edit
3. Click **"Edit"** button
4. Modify details as needed
5. Click **"Save Changes"**

### Account Types

- **Checking**: Daily spending account
- **Savings**: Long-term savings account
- **Credit Card**: Track credit card spending and payments
- **Cash**: Physical cash on hand
- **Investment**: Stocks, bonds, and other investments

## Tracking Income

### Recording Income

1. Click **"+ Add Transaction"** or go to **Income** section
2. Select **"Income"** as transaction type
3. Enter details:
   - **Amount**: Income amount
   - **Account**: Which account received the money
   - **Category**: Type of income (salary, freelance, etc.)
   - **Date**: When you received it
   - **Description**: Optional notes
   - **Recurring**: Set if this is regular income
4. Click **"Save"**

### Income Categories

Default categories include:
- Salary
- Freelance
- Business Income
- Investment Returns
- Gifts
- Other Income

You can customize categories in **Settings → Categories**.

### Recurring Income

For regular income (like salary):

1. Check **"Recurring"** when adding income
2. Set frequency:
   - Weekly
   - Bi-weekly
   - Monthly
   - Quarterly
   - Annually
3. Set start and optional end date
4. Qash Compass will automatically create future transactions

## Recording Expenses

### Adding an Expense

1. Click **"+ Add Transaction"** or go to **Expenses** section
2. Select **"Expense"** as transaction type
3. Enter details:
   - **Amount**: Expense amount
   - **Account**: Which account paid for it
   - **Category**: Type of expense (food, transport, etc.)
   - **Date**: When you spent it
   - **Description**: What you bought
   - **Receipt**: Attach photo (optional)
4. Click **"Save"**

### Expense Categories

Default categories:
- Housing (rent, mortgage, utilities)
- Transportation (gas, public transit)
- Food & Dining (groceries, restaurants)
- Healthcare (medical, insurance)
- Entertainment (movies, hobbies)
- Shopping (clothing, personal items)
- Bills & Utilities
- Education
- Personal Care
- Other

### Attaching Receipts

1. When adding an expense, click **"Attach Receipt"**
2. Select an image file from your computer
3. The receipt will be stored with the transaction
4. View receipts by clicking on the transaction

## Budgets

### Creating a Budget

1. Navigate to **Budgets**
2. Click **"+ New Budget"**
3. Select budget type:
   - **Category Budget**: Budget for specific spending category
   - **Overall Budget**: Total spending limit
4. Enter details:
   - **Category** (if category budget)
   - **Amount**: Budget limit
   - **Period**: Monthly, Quarterly, or Annually
   - **Start Date**: When budget begins
5. Click **"Create Budget"**

### Monitoring Budgets

The Budgets page shows:
- **Progress Bars**: Visual representation of spending vs. budget
- **Percentage Used**: How much of budget is spent
- **Remaining Amount**: Money left in budget
- **Days Remaining**: Time left in budget period
- **Color Coding**:
  - Green: Under 70% of budget
  - Yellow: 70-90% of budget
  - Red: 90-100% of budget
  - Dark Red: Over budget

### Budget Alerts

Receive notifications when:
- You reach 75% of budget
- You reach 90% of budget
- You exceed your budget

Configure alerts in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Reports and Analytics

### Available Reports

1. **Income vs. Expenses**
   - Compare income and expenses over time
   - View by month, quarter, or year

2. **Spending by Category**
   - See where your money goes
   - Identify top spending categories

3. **Net Worth Trend**
   - Track financial growth over time
   - See all account balances combined

4. **Cash Flow**
   - Understand money in vs. money out
   - Identify patterns and trends

5. **Budget Performance**
   - Review budget adherence
   - Compare planned vs. actual spending

### Generating Reports

1. Go to **Reports**
2. Select report type
3. Choose date range
4. Apply filters (accounts, categories)
5. Click **"Generate Report"**
6. View interactive charts and tables

### Exporting Reports

1. Generate the desired report
2. Click **"Export"** button
3. Choose format:
   - **PDF**: For printing or sharing
   - **CSV**: For spreadsheet analysis
   - **Excel**: For advanced analysis
4. Select save location

## Categories

### Managing Categories

1. Go to **Settings → Categories**
2. View income and expense categories
3. Add, edit, or delete categories as needed

### Adding a Category

1. Click **"+ Add Category"**
2. Enter category name
3. Select type (Income or Expense)
4. Choose icon and color
5. Set parent category (for subcategories)
6. Click **"Save"**

### Best Practices

- Keep categories simple and intuitive
- Use subcategories for detailed tracking
- Don't create too many categories
- Review and consolidate unused categories

## Data Management

### Backing Up Data

**Manual Backup:**
1. Go to **File → Backup Data**
2. Choose backup location
3. Click **"Create Backup"**
4. Save the `.qcbackup` file safely

**Automatic Backup:**
1. Go to **Settings → Backup**
2. Enable **"Automatic Backup"**
3. Set backup frequency (daily, weekly, monthly)
4. Choose backup location
5. Set number of backups to keep

### Restoring Data

1. Go to **File → Restore Data**
2. Select `.qcbackup` file
3. Confirm restoration
4. Application will restart with restored data

### Importing Data

Import from other applications:

1. Go to **File → Import**
2. Select import format:
   - CSV (generic)
   - Quicken (QIF)
   - Microsoft Money
3. Map fields to Qash Compass format
4. Review imported data
5. Click **"Complete Import"**

### Exporting Data

1. Go to **File → Export**
2. Choose what to export:
   - All transactions
   - Specific date range
   - Specific accounts
3. Select format (CSV or Excel)
4. Choose save location

## Settings

### General Settings

- **Currency**: Default currency for new transactions
- **Date Format**: How dates are displayed
- **Language**: Interface language (if multiple languages supported)
- **Start Page**: Which page to show on launch

### Security Settings

- **Data Encryption**: Enable/disable encryption
- **Change Password**: Update encryption password
- **Auto-lock**: Lock app after period of inactivity
- **Require Password on Launch**: Extra security

### Display Settings

- **Theme**: Light or Dark mode
- **Font Size**: Adjust for readability
- **Date Format**: Customize date display
- **Number Format**: Decimal separator and thousands separator

### Notification Settings

- **Budget Alerts**: Enable/disable budget warnings
- **Recurring Transaction Reminders**: Get notified about upcoming recurring transactions
- **Low Balance Alerts**: Warning when account balance is low

### Advanced Settings

- **Data Location**: View where data is stored
- **Database Maintenance**: Optimize and repair database
- **Reset Application**: Restore to factory settings (keeps data)

## Tips for Success

1. **Enter Transactions Regularly**: Don't wait too long to record expenses
2. **Use Categories Consistently**: Helps with accurate reporting
3. **Set Realistic Budgets**: Based on historical spending
4. **Review Monthly**: Spend time reviewing your finances each month
5. **Keep Receipts**: Attach digital copies for records
6. **Backup Regularly**: Protect your financial data
7. **Update Accounts**: Keep account balances accurate
